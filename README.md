## Overview
The **Technical Rukwangali Project** is an open-access initiative establishing standardized, university-level STEM terminology, operational taxonomies, and reference lexicons in **Rukwangali** (`ISO 639-3: kwn`).

Historically, Rukwangali reference works have lacked formal definitions, morphological rationales, and modern scientific registers—creating a barrier for educators, students, textbook translators, and NLP researchers. This repository provides freely accessible, machine-readable, and peer-citable papers and lexicons designed to close that gap permanently.

## Core Methodology
* **Morphologically Motivated Coinage:** Operational concepts and structural relations are built from productive Rukwangali verb roots and noun classes (e.g., diminutive `-gona` for sub-structures, reversive/inverse derivations, and Class 7 `si-` for measuring instruments).
* **VIM & International Standards Alignment:** Scientific and metrological terms map directly to international frameworks such as the *International Vocabulary of Metrology (VIM)* so technical distinctions remain rigorous across languages.
* **Pragmatic Borrowing:** Phonological adaptation (e.g., *aldjebra*, *karkurasi*, *atomu*) is reserved strictly for international atomic entities or cases where native roots face semantic overload.
* **Cross-Domain Collision Prevention:** Terminology is engineered across disciplines—metrology, mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, computer science, and philosophy—to avoid overlapping or conflicting coinages.

## Repository Contents

### 1. Technical Lexicons (`/lexicons`)
* **`Registera Zoudivi Wopaundunge, Aldjebra` (v1.0, Nov 2026)** — Bilingual Rukwangali–English lexicon covering Propositional Logic & Proof Theory, Quantifiers & Set Theory, Botanical Algebra & Polynomials, Functions & Calculus, and Linear Algebra, Control Systems & Physics.

### 2. Terminology & Methodology Papers (`/papers`)
* **`Distinguishing Calibration, Adjustment, and Traceability in Technical Rukwangali: A Morphologically Motivated Operational Taxonomy`** — Establishes distinct operational stems for calibration (*epamusiko*), adjustment (*elisitununa*), and metrological traceability (*kuvarera / evarero / uvarero*) aligned with VIM standards.
* *Forthcoming:* Precision, Trueness, Error, and Uncertainty in Technical Rukwangali; Metrology & Measurement Standards Lexicon; Natural Sciences & Computing Registers.

### 3. Plain-Text Datasets (`/data`)
* Structured `.csv` and `.json` exports of all published lexicons for direct integration into spellcheckers, keyboards, digital dictionaries, and African NLP pipelines.
